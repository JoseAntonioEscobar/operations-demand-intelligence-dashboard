# Operations & Demand Intelligence Dashboard

Executive dashboard designed to showcase demand planning, inventory management, operational performance tracking, supply chain visibility, project portfolio management, and business analytics capabilities.

## Live Demo

https://joseantonioescobar.github.io/operations-demand-intelligence-dashboard/

## Overview

This dashboard simulates a modern operations management environment where executives and planners can monitor key business metrics through a unified interface.

The project combines demand planning, inventory analytics, KPI scorecards, supply chain monitoring, project tracking, and process performance measurement into a single interactive dashboard.

## Key Features

### Demand Planning
- Forecast accuracy monitoring
- Demand trend analysis
- Sales and inventory alignment
- Planning performance indicators

### Inventory Management
- Inventory turnover tracking
- Stock coverage monitoring
- Inventory health indicators
- Service level visibility

### Supply Chain Operations
- Supplier performance metrics
- Operational KPI monitoring
- Process efficiency tracking
- Supply chain visibility

### Project Portfolio Management
- Strategic initiative tracking
- Project status monitoring
- Resource allocation visibility
- Progress reporting

### Executive Scorecards
- KPI dashboards
- Operational performance overview
- Business intelligence reporting
- Data-driven decision support
